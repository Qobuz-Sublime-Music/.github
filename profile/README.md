# Qobuz Sublime Music

Qobuz Sublime is a premium music subscription designed for listeners who value high-resolution sound, detailed album information, and direct access to music purchases. It combines Lossless and Hi-Res streaming with offline listening, editorial recommendations, and preferential pricing on eligible Hi-Res albums.

## Overview

Qobuz Sublime combines a high-quality music streaming catalogue with benefits for purchasing eligible music albums. The service focuses on sound quality, music discovery, artist information, and access to digital releases.

Subscribers can explore albums, listen to music in supported audio quality settings, save music for offline playback, and access editorial material that provides additional context about artists and recordings.

## Key Features

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Hi-Res Audio | Stream compatible music in high-resolution FLAC formats up to 24-bit and 192 kHz. |
| Lossless Streaming | Listen to music with lossless audio quality where supported. |
| Music Catalogue | Explore a broad selection of albums, artists, and musical genres. |
| Offline Listening | Save supported music for playback without an active connection through compatible applications. |
| Hi-Res Purchase Discounts | Receive preferential prices on eligible high-resolution album purchases. |
| Music Editorials | Explore articles, album guides, artist features, and curated recommendations. |
| Album Information | Access available credits, digital booklets, and recording details. |
| Personal Library | Organize favourites, playlists, and eligible purchased music. |
| Hi-Fi Integration | Listen through supported audio equipment and compatible playback systems. |

## Hi-Res Streaming Quality

Qobuz supports high-resolution audio playback for compatible recordings and equipment. Supported formats can reach 24-bit and 192 kHz, depending on the release and playback configuration.

Actual output quality depends on the source recording, selected quality settings, application, network conditions, and connected audio equipment.

## Music Purchases and Sublime Benefits

A distinguishing feature of Sublime is its combination of streaming access and preferential prices on eligible Hi-Res music purchases.

The purchase discount varies by album and catalogue availability. Review the displayed price for each release before completing a transaction.

Purchased albums and streaming access are separate parts of the service. Availability for streaming can differ from purchase availability, and subscription terms determine which benefits remain active.

## Music Discovery and Editorial Content

Qobuz provides editorial material alongside its music catalogue. Listeners can explore curated selections, artist profiles, album reviews, and recommendations that provide context beyond the audio itself.

These resources help users discover recordings, investigate musical styles, and explore artists through their discographies.

## Personal Music Library

The Qobuz library brings supported listening and collection features together in one account. Users can organize favourites, manage playlists, revisit albums, and access eligible purchases through compatible applications.

Library features and synchronization behaviour depend on the application and account configuration.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Suggested Check |
| --- | --- |
| Hi-Res playback is unavailable | Check the release quality, application settings, and connected equipment. |
| Audio quality is lower than expected | Review streaming quality settings and the capabilities of the playback device. |
| Offline playback does not work | Confirm that the content has been saved using a supported application. |
| A purchase discount is missing | Check whether the album qualifies for Sublime pricing in your region. |
| An album cannot be streamed | Check regional availability and whether the album is included in the streaming catalogue. |
| Purchased music is not visible | Confirm the signed-in account and review the library or purchase history. |
